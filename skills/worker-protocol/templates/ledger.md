# Ledger

Written by the manager only. Every deferred or found item stays here until it is closed. One line
each, starting with a dash and an open box, then `L<n> <item> [source]`. Close a line by changing
the box to `[x]` with its evidence, or to `[R]` with the reason it was reported to the user.
The work is not done while any line is open.
